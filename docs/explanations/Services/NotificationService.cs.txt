Services/NotificationService.cs — Purpose and key responsibilities

Purpose:
- Business service that orchestrates sending notifications (email and SMS) and persisting in-app notifications.

What it does:
- SendEmailAsync uses injected IEmailSender to send messages and returns ApiResponse with success/failure details.
- SendSmsAsync composes an HTTP request to the configured SMS provider using injected HttpClient.
- Other methods (e.g., GetByCustomerAsync, SendAsync overloads) are currently unimplemented and should be completed.

Usage:
- Called by AuthService to send registration OTPs and by other services when notifications are required.
