DTOs/UserDto.cs — Purpose and key responsibilities

Purpose:
- Safe, public representation of user data returned by the API.

Fields included:
- Id, Email, FirstName, LastName, Phone, Department, ProfileImageUrl, StaffId, Role, RegistrationOtp (if used), IsActive, CreatedAt, LastLoginAt, Accounts (list of AccountDto).

Usage:
- Created by AuthService.MapToDto and returned in ApiResponse<UserDto>. Avoid exposing sensitive fields like PasswordHash in DTOs.
