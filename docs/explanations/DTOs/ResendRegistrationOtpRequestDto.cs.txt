DTOs/ResendRegistrationOtpRequestDto.cs — Purpose and key responsibilities

Purpose:
- Payload used when a client requests the server to resend the registration OTP.

Typical fields:
- Email or Phone: identifier to find the user and resend OTP.

Usage:
- Bound in AuthController.ResendRegistrationOtp and passed to AuthService.ResendRegistrationOtpAsync.

Notes:
- Rate-limit OTP resends to prevent abuse.
