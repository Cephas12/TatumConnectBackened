DTOs/VerifyRegistrationOtpRequestDto.cs — Purpose and key responsibilities

Purpose:
- Payload used to verify the one-time password (OTP) during registration.

Typical fields:
- Email (or Phone) and Otp: the identifier and the code to verify.

Usage:
- Bound in AuthController.Verify and passed to AuthService.VerifyRegistrationOtpAsync which verifies OTP and activates the user's account if valid.

Notes:
- Validate OTP expiry and decrement remaining attempt count to mitigate brute-force attempts.
