DTOs/AccountDto.cs — Purpose and key responsibilities

Purpose:
- Represents account information sent to clients in API responses.

Typical fields:
- Id, AccountNumber, Name, Currency, LedgerBalance, AvailableBalance, Status, CreatedAt, IsActive.

Usage:
- Included in UserDto.Accounts list so client can view a user's accounts without exposing internal DB details.

Notes:
- Keep DTOs minimal and avoid exposing internal identifiers unless needed.
